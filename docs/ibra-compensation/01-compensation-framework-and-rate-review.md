Loblaw's current fully-loaded cost for Ibra is IG's bill rate, estimated at $57.50–$80.50/hr ($46 × 1.25–1.75), i.e. **$120K–$167K/yr**.

**Interpretation.**
- **$180–200K base from a Loblaw conversion: not realistic.** It is above every IC band Loblaw posts and 8–50% above what Loblaw pays for Ibra today. A conversion lands on an existing IC band, not a Director band. Probability: low.

- **Any added duties attached to a conversion are a level argument (Lead, not Senior), not a reason to accept a Senior band.** Keep this out of recruiter-facing text.

## 4. Two-job replacement math

**Observation.** $46 × 40 + $53 × 32 = $1,840 + $1,696 = **$3,536/wk** over 72 hrs = **$49.11/hr blended**. Annual: × 50 = $176,800; × 52 = $183,872.

| 36 hrs | $98.22/hr (the "$99 equivalent") | ~$114/hr | same |

Add 5–10% for single-employer concentration risk → **$155–163K base (~$185–194K package)**.

**Interpretation: above or below market?**
- As a **product-design contract**, $88–98/hr is the top of the senior band ($80–100) and below principal ($98.80–114.40). Achievable, at senior-plus or principal scope.
- As a **digital/motion/web contract** (how Ibra is coded today), $88–98/hr is **above market** ($60–79). The replacement number is not reachable under the current role label.
- As a **salary**, $155–163K is above Loblaw's posted ladder (max $154K) and at the floor of the Staff bands (Coursera $147.6K, Samsara $161.6K, Jobber $169.2K). Above median, inside top-quartile evidence.
- **So:** one engagement that replaces both jobs exists in the market, but only as senior-plus/principal product design, Lead/Director in a product org, or senior brand/motion at a premium studio. The IG review is step one, not the destination.
