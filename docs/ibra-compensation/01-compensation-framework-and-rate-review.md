## 3. Framework table

Targets reflect Lead/Principal scope (end-to-end ownership of SHN, cross-functional lead, design-system decisions across PC Masterbrand and SHN). Walk-away = the number below which the role is a bridge and the job search goes active, not the number at which Ibra quits.

| | **T4 hourly (via agency, e.g. IG)** | **Incorporated / freelance hourly (direct)** | **Permanent base** | **Permanent total package** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | **$105/hr** | **$125/hr** | **$185,000** | **~$220,000** |
| **Target** | **$95/hr** | **$110/hr** | **$170,000** | **~$200,000** |
| **Walk-away** | **$88/hr** | **$95/hr** | **$155,000** | **~$185,000** |

**Arithmetic and evidence**

- *T4 hourly.* Senior band $80–100 (#2, #4); principal band $98.80–114.40 (#1). Opening $105 sits in the lower third of the principal band. Target $95 is the gap between senior top ($90) and principal floor ($98.80). Walk-away $88 = the two-job replacement rate at 40 hrs ($3,500 ÷ 40 = $87.50). Annualised: $95 × 2,000 = $190K; $88 × 2,000 = $176K.
- *Incorporated / direct.* No agency margin, but Ibra carries both halves of CPP (~$4–8K), no 4% ESA vacation pay, HST admin, accounting (~$2–3K) and gap risk. Rule of thumb: T4 rate × 1.15–1.20. $95 × 1.16 ≈ $110 target; $105 × 1.19 ≈ $125 opening; $88 × 1.08 ≈ $95 floor (floor stays tight because direct gigs are usually shorter). Adobe at $115 (2022) corroborates the target. An agency paying $114.40 (#1) is billing the client an estimated $140–170 at 25–50% markup (#31), so a direct principal engagement at $120–135 is inside what clients already pay.
- *Permanent base.* Opening $185K ≈ Samsara midpoint ($192K) and just under Jobber's midpoint ($199K), where Jobber says established hires start (#12). Target $170K sits inside Samsara, Jobber and Coursera bands (#12–14) and above Loblaw's Lead ceiling. Walk-away $155K = contract parity at $88/hr: ($176K − $5K) ÷ 1.16 ≈ $147K, plus ~5% for giving up contract flexibility, rounded to Loblaw's Lead top ($154K).
- *Permanent total package.* Each base × 1.16 + $5K: $185K → $219.6K; $170K → $202K; $155K → $184.8K. Ibra's stated $180–200K floor is honest as a **total package** target; as a **base** target it only fits the Staff/Principal tier in §1c.

## 6. Call outline for AJ (10 bullets)

1. **Open with scope evidence, three items, before/after.** Original brief: Senior PD on one workstream. Now: end-to-end design lead on SHN, cross-functional owner (content, dev, partners), design-system decisions spanning PC Masterbrand and SHN. Bring one page of dated examples; offer to send it to the hiring manager.
2. **The ask, stated once, with a number.** "Reset the engagement to Lead/Principal scope and move the rate to $95/hr (open at $105 if AJ asks for a number to take to the client). Market for principal product design contracts in Ontario is $98.80–$114.40 through Robert Half right now; senior is $80–100."
3. **Objection: "The client's budget is fixed."** Response: "Budgets are set per role level. What's the client's approved band for a Lead or Principal on this team? If this scope is a level above what was budgeted, that's a scope change, and I'll document it so the manager can take it through approvals."
4. **Objection: "The rate was agreed."** Response: "Agreed for the original scope. The scope changed, so this is a review, not a renegotiation. The extension/renewal date is the natural checkpoint, so let's lock the adjustment to that date."
5. **Objection: "We can't change mid-contract."** Response: ask for the exact extension date and agree the new rate takes effect then; ask whether a one-time true-up for the months of expanded scope is possible.
6. **Ask about IG's bill rate and spread.** "What's the bill rate to Loblaw on my role, and what's IG's margin on it? If the client approves an increase, how much of it lands on my side?" AJ may decline to share the bill rate; still ask whether IG will fund part of the increase from its margin (industry markups run 25–75%), since a client-side "no" doesn't have to be the end.
7. **Ask about the client's approval band and who signs.** Who approves (hiring manager, procurement, VP), what the band is for the next level up, and whether IG has a comparable Lead/Principal placed at Loblaw today.
8. **Timeline.** Ask AJ to take it to the Loblaw manager within one week and set a date for the answer. Confirm what Ibra should send (scope one-pager) and by when.
9. **What Ibra does not raise.** Full-time conversion, Kobo, past Adobe/Scout rates. If AJ raises conversion, park it: "Happy to talk about that separately; today is about the current engagement."
10. **Close in writing.** Same day, email AJ a summary: the scope evidence, the ask ($95/hr, Lead/Principal), the agreed next step and date.
