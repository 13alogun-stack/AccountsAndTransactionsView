## 1. Verdict

1. **Proves today:** senior-level visual, brand and motion craft in agency settings (Huge, DEPT, Scout Lab, Mirror, CTHDRL), plus one credible product/systems case (PC Masterbrand). Supports: Senior Visual/Brand Designer, Motion Designer, Senior Designer at a studio.
2. **Does not yet prove:** Principal or Lead product design. No case shows a decision Ibra made that other designers, engineers or stakeholders followed, no trade-off, no outcome, no research, no metric. Ownership language is inflated in two places (Chromebook Plus on About and in the deck) and vague in the one that matters most (PC Masterbrand).
3. **Blocks the $180K+ / $95+/hr argument:** the site and deck disagree on titles and dates for 4 of 6 employers, and the two credentials recruiters check first (current title, Webby) appear in four different wordings.
4. **Cannot be assessed from here:** visual quality, the carousel, mobile, motion. That is a real gap in this audit, not a pass.
5. **One-line fix path:** make ownership explicit on four cases, make every title/date identical across site, deck and LinkedIn, add one product/systems case with a decision and an outcome. Keep the black canvas, white type, orange accent and the carousel untouched.
